For T85 (continues T83). The designs and numbers come from W81's investigation, run 20261108-011445-085-investigation, answer.md. This page only renders them; no inbox code changed.

What the evidence says

  • Noticing is slow. Median time to a reply is 16 min, and 6 of 21 replies took over 2 h. Nothing notifies you about a new ask; the push listener exists only while the inbox buffer does.
  • Under half of asks get answered in the inbox. Of 55 asks in 6 days, 21 were replied to there. The rest were answered by a Router steer, a cancel, or overtaken when the Worker finished.
  • You triage in batches. o820, o826 and T39 pasted several asks into the Router at once, or said "decide yourselves".
  • The current outline is cluttered: 83 top-level headings, 67 of them ARCHIVED, and landing boilerplate repeated on three done threads.

Shared fixes, whichever design you pick (cheap): one folded ARCHIVED heading; done threads collapse to one line without landing boilerplate; "since you last looked" from the existing per-client cursors (inbox.edn :cursors); a notification when an ask opens.

Mocks are drawn for the 210×59 tty frame, scaled down. Threads T72, T77, T80, T82, T84 and T85, their landings and the 67 archived threads come from the live bbh inbox org on 2026-11-08. T72 a53 and T82 a55 are real earlier asks replayed as open. T85 a56 stands in for the question this page leads to; its id and times are made up.

A new question arrives and is answered

1. Ask arrives

W83 files a56 on T85. It joins the end of the queue; the header goes from "1 of 2" to "1 of 3". The screen never jumps while you are on another question.

2. You reach it

After answering T72 a53 with 2, the queue advances to T85 a56 with the cursor on the recommended option.

3. Answer

. sends option 2. The footer shows "sent · u undo" for 10 s, then the next ask (T82 a55) appears.

4. Done

When the queue is empty the buffer shows the since-you-looked footer and q returns to the outline.

Overview it gives

Thin. A one-line "since you last looked" footer plus the queue of what is next. You need the outline (one key away, q) or another design for "what is happening".

Build cost on top of T82

S–M, about 1–2 days. Reuses T82's pick!, answer!, send! and draft. New: a queue order (stopped first, then oldest), one renderer for a single ask, a send-then-advance wrapper. Undo needs a delayed outbox, which is M on its own.

Tradeoffs

  • Fastest flow when 3 or more asks wait.
  • Matches the batch-triage habit, including "decide yourself".
  • Works the same at 80 columns and on the phone.
  • Loses context across asks on the same thread.
  • Useless for overview; needs another view alongside.
  • Still relies on you opening it: no help with noticing.

A new question arrives and is answered

1. Ask arrives

A T85 card appears in NEEDS YOU, the a56 heading is added under the board, and the header says "since 11:02: +2 asks". Point does not move.

2. You see it

On the next glance the column count reads 3. ]c jumps to the a56 heading.

4. Done

The card moves from NEEDS YOU back to WORKING when W83 resumes.

Overview it gives

The best at a glance: who is working, waiting, stuck and done, without folding anything.

Build cost on top of T82

M, about 2–3 days. bbh inbox org gains a server-rendered text board on a 210-column grid with column math, and a stacked fallback under about 120 columns. Card navigation is new elisp.

Tradeoffs

  • Dense and readable on the 210×59 frame.
  • Answers "what is everyone doing" in one look.
  • Poor at 80 columns; the phone needs a separate layout.
  • Box drawing is not org: no folding or org search on the board.
  • Highest cost, and still needs notify for new asks.

Build cost on top of T82

M, about 2–3 days. New small list buffer, selection sync, two-window layout (an exception to T82's "each view takes the whole frame"), a debounced thread call per selection, and an 80-column fallback.

  • Most mail-client-like; best for one thread with long context.
  • No new chords: reuses window focus.
  • Two windows squeeze a tty frame; degrades to a list at 80×25.
  • More state to keep correct across pushes (selection, two buffers).
  • Needs notify for new asks.

A new question arrives and is answered

1. Ask arrives

The controller's dispatcher runs notify-send and a Herdr toast. The mode line turns to ● bbh 3 ask. A tty child frame (Emacs 31) shows the ask for a few seconds without taking keys, even while you type in the Router.

2. You pick it up

SPC a a focuses the popup. Or on the phone the title badge vibrates; or by voice, "answer: two".

3. Answer

2 sends. n goes to T72 a53 next; q returns you to your buffer.

4. Long context

If Details don't fit, o opens the full view (any of 1–4).

Overview it gives

Minimal: three counts. It relies on a full view for context.

Build cost on top of T82

S + M. S (about half a day) for the counter and notify-send. M (about 2 days) for the popup: a global push listener (today it lives only with the inbox buffer), a bbh inbox next --edn verb, and child-frame testing on tty.

Tradeoffs

  • Attacks the real problem: median 16 min to reply, 6 of 21 over 2 h, nothing notifies today.
  • Stay in flow; works from any buffer, phone or voice.
  • Interrupts by nature; must never grab focus or keys while you type.
  • Long Details don't fit; it is for quick picks.
  • No overview on its own.

Recommendation

Build 5 + 1 on top of 3, after the shared fixes.

The data says the problem is noticing (median 16 min, 6 of 21 over 2 h, nothing notifies) and batch triage (o820, o826, T39 pasted into the Router). Design 5 fixes noticing cheaply. Design 1 is the batch-triage flow, including "decide yourself". Design 3 is the cheapest real overview, because it is one query over events that already exist. Designs 2 and 4 look best at 210 columns, but they cost the most and break at 80 columns and on the phone.
