Judge only the outcome for the person: did the replay reach the brief's goal at least as well (correct, complete, verified, honestly reported), and at what cost (tool calls, errors, tokens, minutes)? A replay that reaches the same result with clearly less cost is better. A replay that is cheaper but misses part of the goal, or reports unverified claims, is worse. Metrics alone do not decide; a report that claims success without evidence counts against its run.

Answer in two parts. First a critique of at most five sentences that names the decisive difference. Then a last line that is exactly one of:
VERDICT: better
VERDICT: same
VERDICT: worse
