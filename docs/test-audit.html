How the skill was applied

The skill is https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/tree/main/.agents/skills/test-audit: SKILL.md, references/audit.md and CAMPAIGN.md, plus the testing docs they link. It is instructions only and ships no scripts. Its method:

  • Junk patterns: duplicate contracts, copied inventories, test-only production seams, mocks or fixtures that supply the behaviour under test, negatives that cannot fail, and names that promise more than the test exercises.
  • Retention bar: slow or static is not a reason to delete.
  • Ledger: every declaration gets a mark: R keep, F fix, C consolidate, D delete.
  • Evidence: no deletion without naming the proof that remains.
  • Validation: a deliberate mutation shows that the remaining proof catches the bug.

I split the tests into seven areas, each read in full by a separate read-only reviewer that wrote a ledger. I checked every candidate against the source before editing it. The ledgers are in /home/engstrand/.local/state/bbh/workflows/20261116-143746-670-change/evidence/ledgers

Checked and kept

  • Some bbh.super/start! options are passed only by tests: :launch, :sessions and the clock and scan options. They keep a test controller from reaping your real sessions, and nothing cheaper does that.
  • vimium-cljs's test-page? data attributes are the only way the e2e tests can see the mode and hints. They only switch on when a fixture page opts in.
  • The EXT_RELOAD_* environment variables keep the ext-reload test away from your live hub and Chrome profile.
  • The timing tests in agent-shell-test guard real performance contracts, including the link regexp that once hung the daemon.
  • All ten lint steps can fail, and every checks.edn rule names files and commands that exist.
